Save / load

A 3×3 is saved as the same 54 letters Claude's set_cube takes; other sizes start with the size, e.g. 4:UUU….

Key bindings

Click a key, then press the new one (Esc unbinds). Hold Shift for counter-clockwise. Type a number first for inner layers: 3 R turns the 3rd layer from the right, 3 Rw turns 3 layers.

Mouse / touch

Drag a sticker to turn its layer. Right- or middle-drag to orbit (on touch: drag the background), scroll or pinch to zoom. Click a move in the history (or a step of Claude's plan) to go back or forward to it.
